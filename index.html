About us

//

The workshop behind the MrNiceGuy channel.

Most of our customers find us through the MrNiceGuy YouTube channel, and they all come for the same thing: results. A Mazda 2.2 Skyactiv-D that's fixed properly and runs the way it should — from customers right across the UK and Europe.

We don't do guesswork. A warning light on the dash only tells you something's wrong, and a fault code only points in a direction — the same code can have a dozen different causes. That's why every job starts with a proper diagnostic, where we go by the live data readings, not hunches.

Too far away to pop in? We also offer online consultations for a fee — all the know-how, minus the oily handshake. Just drop us a message.
